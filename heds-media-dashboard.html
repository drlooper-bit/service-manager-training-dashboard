Due Diligence & Operational Inspections

We walk your floor, pull your ERP reports, and show you exactly where the cash is leaving. Not a desktop review — a full onsite operational audit of your service, parts, and rental departments.

What We Audit

Service Department

  • WIP aging and days-to-invoice
  • Labor recovery and unbilled hours
  • Effective labor rate vs. posted rate
  • Quote follow-up and close rate
  • Warranty recovery rate
  • First-time fix rate
  • Absorption contribution

Parts Department

  • Fill rate and backorder exposure
  • Dead stock and aging inventory
  • Stock order vs. emergency order ratio
  • Gross profit margin on parts
  • Bin accuracy and receiving discipline
  • Physical inventory procedures
  • Master data cleanliness in ERP

Rental Fleet

  • Time utilization rate
  • Dollar utilization rate
  • Off-rent turnaround time
  • Downtime percentage
  • Maintenance cost as % of OEC
  • PM compliance rate
  • Rent-ready status accuracy

Branch Operations

  • Absorption rate (Service + Parts + Rental GP ÷ Fixed Overhead)
  • Cross-functional accountability
  • Daily operating rhythms
  • Scorecard discipline and review cadence
  • Working capital tied up in WIP and inventory
  • EBITDA quality and normalized margin
  • Branch-to-branch consistency

How the Inspection Works

1

Pre-Work

Client pulls 4 standard ERP/DMS reports. HEDS builds a baseline snapshot before arriving onsite.

2

Onsite Audit

1-3 days onsite. Shop floor walk, parts counter review, rental yard inspection, ERP report verification, manager interviews.

3

Findings Report

Detailed report with dollar-quantified leaks, prioritized fix list, and recommended next steps. Delivered within 5 business days.

What You Get

  • Operational findings report with dollar-quantified revenue leaks
  • Department-by-department scorecard showing current state vs. industry benchmarks
  • Prioritized fix list ranked by dollar impact and ease of implementation
  • WIP aging analysis with specific hold reasons and dollar exposure
  • Labor reconciliation showing unbilled hours and dollar exposure
  • Parts inventory health snapshot (fill rate, dead stock, aging)
  • Rental fleet utilization and downtime analysis
  • Recommendation for training engagement if behavioral gaps are driving the leaks

EBITDA Quality & Acquisition Due Diligence

For PE-backed equipment groups, dealers, or rental companies evaluating an acquisition or preparing for sale:

  • Normalized EBITDA quality assessment — are the earnings real or are they inflated by deferred maintenance and WIP leakage?
  • Working capital analysis — how much cash is trapped in aged WIP and dead inventory?
  • Operational risk scoring across service, parts, and rental departments
  • Post-acquisition integration plan — what to fix first and in what order
  • Branch-level consistency audit — are all locations operating to the same standard?
  • Management capability assessment — can the current team execute the plan?

Track 2: Parts & Inventory Discipline

For: Parts Managers, Warehouse Leads, Purchasing
Duration: 3 days onsite (24 hours) | Price: $4,800 (up to 12 participants)

Parts discipline is not about pricing strategy — it is about control. Fill rate drives service throughput. Dead stock ties up working capital. Receiving errors cause inventory adjustments that hide real losses. This track installs the daily rhythms that keep parts available and inventory clean.

ModuleFocus
1 — Parts Manager Finance & Fill RateFill-rate definition and measurement, stock vs. emergency order policy, vendor accountability scorecard, backorder drift limits
2 — Physical Inventory & Receiving DisciplinePhysical inventory procedures, receiving checklist (PO match, put-away within 24h, BIN verification), controlled adjustment policy
3 — Dead Stock & Obsolescence ControlNo-move identification, obsolescence review cadence, disposition options (return, scrap, bulk sale), ERP master-data cleanup
4 — Parts Constraint Impact on Service & RentalHow parts shortages block service bays and rent-ready status, cross-functional escalation, constraint impact dollar score
5 — Purchasing & Vendor Management DisciplinePO approval matrix by dollar tier, min/max and reorder point discipline, freight policy, return policy and vendor RMA process

Core KPIs: Fill Rate %, Emergency Order Count, Backorder Aging, Inventory Adjustment $, Count Accuracy %, Dead Stock %, Inventory Turns, Parts Constraint Impact Score

Track 3: Rental Fleet & Uptime

For: Rental Managers, Fleet Managers, Fleet Coordinators
Duration: 3 days onsite (24 hours) | Price: $4,800 (up to 12 participants)

The fleet is the revenue-generating asset. Every day a machine sits in the shop instead of on rent, the company loses the daily rental rate. This track installs the workflows that get machines back on rent fast — and keep them earning.

ModuleFocus
1 — Rent-Ready Workflow DesignRent-ready checklist (mechanical, cosmetic, attachment, documentation), off-rent-to-repair trigger within 4 hours of return, repair planning alignment
2 — Attachment & Asset ControlEvery attachment has ERP assignment, contract linkage, and condition photos at check-out and check-in, damage documentation, return inspection checklist
3 — Maintenance Cost vs. OEC & Telematics Alert ResponseMaintenance cost benchmark as % of OEC by asset class, deferred maintenance exposure, telematics alert response protocol, mixed-OEM fault code framework
4 — Downtime Value & Fleet Earning RiskDowntime value calculation (daily rental rate × expected rental days lost), fleet earning risk score, shop priority rule: highest downtime value wins the bay
5 — Rental Rate & Utilization DisciplineRate review cadence and market benchmark, utilization floor rule, rental contract checklist (insurance, damage deposit, inspection photos, attachment list)

Core KPIs: Rent-Ready Failure Risk, Off-Rent-to-Ready Time, Asset Availability %, Maintenance Cost as % of OEC, PM Compliance %, Dollar Utilization %, Downtime Value, Attachment Mismatch Count
